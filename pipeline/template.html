Definition: a match qualifies when both teams scored in the 1st half AND both teams scored in the 2nd half (90-minute score; extra time and penalties ignored).

Exclusions: matches without a recorded half-time score (≈6,400 across the dataset) cannot be classified and are excluded from rates. Percentages are qualifying matches ÷ matches with HT/FT recorded.

Source: openfootball/football.json (GitHub) · data updated — · Dashboard works fully offline — all data embedded · auto-refreshed from the source daily via a scheduled rebuild (see repo README).

Team focus: H2H and per-team stats use the packed full match history of the selected league (all seasons). Matches without half-time scores are shown as “n/a” and never count as qualifying. Each panel flags the team's top both-halves rival — the opponent they have recorded the most qualifying matches against — and highlights those rows.

Next-meeting outlook: a statistical estimate of the chance that the next match is a BTTS-both-halves game, built from venue-adjusted team rates (75%), head-to-head history (25%) and the league base rate — with small samples pulled toward the league average. It reflects historical frequencies only and is not a guarantee of any future result.
